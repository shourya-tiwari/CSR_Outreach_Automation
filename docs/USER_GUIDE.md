# CSR Outreach Portal — Quick Start Guide

A one-page guide for NGO staff. No technical knowledge needed.

## What this app does

Helps you find companies to approach for CSR partnerships, find the
right person to contact there, keep track of every company and contact
in one place, and write outreach emails faster — all from one screen at
a time.

## The five screens (left-hand menu)

| Screen | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your homepage. Totals (companies, replies, meetings, proposals), what follow-ups are due or overdue, and a feed of recent activity. |
| **Companies** | Search and filter companies. Click one to open its full profile. |
| **Company Detail** | Everything about one company: info, contacts, notes, documents, proposals, lead score, and the "Generate Email" button. |
| **NGO Profile** | Your organisation's own details (name, mission, focus areas, location). Fill this in once — it's used automatically when scoring leads and writing emails. |

## A typical workflow, start to finish

1. **Search** — go to Companies, filter by city/industry/CSR focus, and
   open one that looks promising.
2. **Check the Lead Score** — every company shows a score (0–100) and a
   High/Medium/Low badge. Click "Why?" for a plain-language explanation.
3. **Find a contact** — click "Scan website" on the company page to look
   for public contact details. Review the results and click "Add
   contact" for the ones you want to keep — nothing is saved
   automatically.
4. **Generate an email** — pick First Outreach / Follow-up / Meeting
   Request / Thank You, click Generate, then read and edit it before
   clicking Copy. **The app never sends anything for you** — you paste
   it into your own email and send it yourself.
5. **Update status** — after you reach out, set the company's status
   (New → Contacted → Follow-up → Meeting → Proposal Sent → Successful /
   Not Interested) and, if relevant, a follow-up date.
6. **Add notes** — jot down anything from a call or meeting, right on
   the company's page.
7. **Track a proposal** — if you send a formal proposal, add it under
   Proposals and move it through its stages as it progresses.
8. **Upload documents** — keep the actual proposal file, a CSR report,
   or a signed MoU attached to the company.
9. **Tag companies** — add free-text tags like "High Priority" or a
   focus area, then filter by tag later to find them again.
10. **Check the Dashboard** — anytime, to see overdue follow-ups and
    what's happened recently across every company.

## A few important rules

- **Emails are always drafts.** Nothing is ever sent automatically —
  you review and send every email yourself.
- **Only public information is collected.** Contact details come from
  public sources (company websites, public profiles) — never private
  phone numbers or personal emails.
- **Duplicates are caught, not silently created.** If you try to add a
  company that already exists (same name or website), the app warns you
  and lets you open the existing one instead.
- **Deleting a company deletes everything under it** — its contacts,
  notes, documents, and proposals. There's no undo, so double-check
  before confirming a delete.

## Importing/exporting your existing list

On the Companies page: **Export** downloads your current filtered list
as a spreadsheet (CSV). **Import** lets you upload a CSV of companies —
rows that look like duplicates of what's already saved are skipped
automatically, and any row with a problem is reported to you instead of
silently failing.

## If something looks wrong

If a screen is confusing or something doesn't work as expected, note
exactly what you were trying to do and what happened — that's the most
useful thing you can hand back to whoever maintains the app.
